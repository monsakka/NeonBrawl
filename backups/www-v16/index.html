How to play

Controls

  • ADMove · double-tap to dash
  • SpaceJump / multi-jump · hold to hover or fly (Solaris, Mecha)
  • Left clickLight attack · click again after a hit to combo
  • Right clickSmash / heavy attack · hold to charge
  • QSpecial attack / recovery
  • EShield · tap it just before a hit = parry
  • RUltimate · when the ULT meter is full (also middle click / ULT button). Fill it with long combos, perfect parries and by surviving at high damage
  • E+ADDodge roll (while shielding)
  • SFast-fall / drop through platforms · Chimera crawls
  • GHTaunts: the two 3D emotes you mapped in Settings → Profile (on the ground; you're open while they play)
  • 1–8Pop the holographic 3D emojis you mapped to those keys (more from the Battle Pass and the Item Shop)
  • EscPause
  • TouchLeft thumb: stick to move and aim · right thumb: on-screen buttons

Fighting

  • Aim decides direction. Cursor above you = up attack, below = down attack, to the side = side attack, on top of you = neutral. Q aims too.
  • Aerials: in the air, light attacks become a spin, a forward hammer, a back kick (aim behind), an up flip-kick or a spiking down stomp. Attack while dashing = slide kick; while walking = thrust.
  • Damage % raises knockback. Launch = base + damage × multiplier, divided by weight. Get launched past a blastzone and you lose a stock.
  • Directional influence: hold A or D while launched to steer toward the stage.
  • Tech: tap E just before you hit the ground or a wall in hit-stun to recover safely. Hold A or D as you land to tech-roll.
  • Shield shrinks while held and shatters if you hold it too long. A parry stuns the attacker and reflects projectiles.
  • Ledges: fall beside the stage edge to grab on, then Space to jump, A/D to climb or E to roll in.
  • Every fighter has a different Q: dashes, teleports, projectiles, grabs, decoys, walls, dive-kicks, updrafts, black holes, rewinds and more. Point the cursor where you want it to go (Mecha, Volt and Blaze: aim straight up to recover).
